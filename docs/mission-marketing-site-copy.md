## Mission Section

Legacy Circle exists to carry J'Mel Dowdell's values forward in living form. We are not building a monument. We are building a mission young people can practice every day.

Through stories, missions, badges, and peer encouragement, students do more than consume content. They build character, strengthen decision-making, and learn how to leave a positive mark on their communities.
